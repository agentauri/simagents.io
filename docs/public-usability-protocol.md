# Public usability gate — five new people

Status: unexecuted. No participants have been contacted or counted. Automated browser sessions and simulated agents are not human usability or beta-session evidence.

Freeze the clean staging candidate before recruiting. Record commit/candidate ID, SPA/relay/admission/catalog/schema versions, public destinations, operator, participant instructions and the exact task order. Recruitment/messages and any provider spending require their separate authorization. Give participants explicit cost/token/request limits and explain any dedicated test-key provision before an inference is sent.

Use five people who have not previously used the product. Include both English and Italian and at least two mobile sessions. Record a pseudonymous study ID, language, device/browser/zoom, familiarity declaration, task start/end and whether assistance occurred. Do not record provider keys, prompts, responses, full payload-bearing stacks, email addresses or raw network addresses in the study log.

Ask each person to:

1. Connect a provider through the chosen direct/public relay route and identify who receives its key/prompts/responses. Complete any security check without an invitation/account.
2. Verify the exact model, identify that this request may be billed, and distinguish tab verification from catalog certification.
3. Choose a scenario, set request/time/output limits and identify that unknown prices are unavailable, not zero or a guaranteed invoice cap.
4. Start the world, identify the live controls, pause, resume explicitly and explain the difference between a user pause and an error stop.
5. Interpret a controlled error and recover access/configuration without expecting fallback or automatic inference retry.
6. Reload and find the saved world, then restore it through explicit key entry/vault unlock and review. Explain which data stays on the device and which goes to a provider/relay.

The moderator observes silently during configuration/start. An instruction repeated, suggested click, key/route/model correction or explanation of an error counts as assistance. Record the exact obstacle rather than rescuing it and calling the task successful. Keep the observer's notes separate from participant answers.

The gate passes only when at least four of five complete configuration and startup without assistance, both languages and two mobile attempts are represented, and no blocking usability/accessibility defect remains. Also record comprehension of charges, data recipients and pause/error stops; successful clicks alone do not prove comprehension. If fewer than four pass, fix the obstacles and repeat with five new people. Preserve the original failed denominator/results.

Suggested record columns: candidate ID, study ID, new-to-product confirmed, language, device/browser/zoom, unaided configuration/start yes/no, assistance details, time, task outcomes, cost explanation, data-recipient explanation, pause/error explanation, reload outcome, observed obstacle, severity and follow-up evidence. Completion and comprehension must be reported separately. Use a local artifact or authorized project system; no automatic content telemetry is introduced.

Public beta evidence is a separate gate: at least seven real days after account-free/invitation-free opening, at least 20 completed sessions from five distinct humans, no critical/high issue open, and a verified compatible rollback within 15 minutes. Relay subjects/HMAC address counters cannot prove distinct humans. Define the completion qualification before beta and retain consented observations or self-reports with aggregate counters, never keys/content. Calendar time or bot sessions alone cannot promote to stable.
